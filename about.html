About Us

Who are we?

Hack Club is a global network of diverse computer science clubs where members learn to code and create amazing things. It’s by the students, for the students—we teach each other how to code and make things through tinkering and building projects. Everyone has a place at our Club, regardless of whether they are an artist, athlete, or musician.

What does this Club give you?

For starters, you'll get tons of free, amazingly awesome swag! In addition to the global Hack Club Community, you will be able to connect with an amazing alumni network. We will also help you get volunteer opportunities, connections with local tech companies, and priority registration at local tech events.

Why should you join us?

Creating is the closest thing that we have to a superpower. Our generation will be the one to deal with pressing issues, like poverty and climate change (it exists, we promise), and engineering enables you to go out and solve those problems. Oh, you don't know how to code or make things? No problem. We will walk you through the ropes from step one.
